## Scope (once approved)
- New journey state after a confirmed **birth** outcome only: the user explicitly opts in to postpartum/baby features. Never auto-enter after loss/termination. Cycle history is preserved; return to cycle tracking with low-confidence predictions (PRD §4.1).
- **Baby profile(s)**: local-first, minimal fields (nickname, birth date, sex optional, gestational age at birth optional). Multiple babies supported.
- **Feeding log**: breast (left/right timer), bottle (amount), pumping. **Diaper** and **sleep** logs. Quick-log widgets.
- **Growth**: weight/length/head circumference with charts against **WHO Child Growth Standards** (0–24 months). Bundled reference tables require licence verification and an ADR. Percentile display is informational with reviewed copy, not diagnosis.
- **Developmental milestones to 5 years**: CMS `milestone_set` (reviewed) as a checklist with "talk to your clinician if" guidance from CMS.
- **Vaccination schedule**: CMS-governed Iran national schedule as reminders. Educational only.
- **Postpartum self-care**: mood check-ins with reviewed postpartum-depression escalation content, and pelvic-floor programs (reuse M17).
- Privacy: baby data is health data; same protections, export and deletion.
