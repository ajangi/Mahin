# M19 Cloud Agent Prompt — Postpartum, Breastfeeding & Baby Growth

> **GATED.** Do not implement until the product owner approves plan §7 item 1 and the PRD is amended (§4 state machine, §5 information architecture, §19 data model). If unapproved, STOP and report.

## Authority
Follow `AGENTS.md`, the amended `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/MEDICAL_CONTENT_GOVERNANCE.md`, `docs/PROGRAM_V2_PLAN.md`. Read the latest handoff (M18b). This prompt scopes the current implementation; the PRD remains authoritative.

## Scope (once approved)
- A new journey state entered only after a confirmed **birth** outcome, and only when the user explicitly opts in. Never entered after loss or termination. Cycle history is preserved, and returning to cycle tracking uses low-confidence predictions (PRD §4.1).
- **Baby profile(s)**: local-first, minimal fields (nickname, birth date, sex optional, gestational age at birth optional). Multiple babies supported.
- **Feeding log**: breast (left/right timer), bottle (amount) and pumping. **Diaper** and **sleep** logs. Quick-log entries on Today.
- **Growth**: weight, length and head circumference charted against the **WHO Child Growth Standards** (0–24 months). Bundled reference tables need licence verification and an ADR. Percentiles are informational, with reviewed copy, and are never a diagnosis.
- **Developmental milestones to 5 years**: CMS `milestone_set` (reviewed), shown as a checklist with CMS-authored "talk to your clinician if" guidance.
- **Vaccination schedule**: CMS-governed Iran national schedule as reminders. Educational only.
- **Postpartum self-care**: mood check-ins with reviewed postpartum-depression escalation content, and pelvic-floor programs reusing M17.

## Execution rules
- Implement **M19 only**, and only after the gate is cleared.
- Baby data is health data: same encryption, redaction, export and deletion guarantees as maternal data.
- Sync/export semantics for baby data must be specified in `SYNC_SPEC.md` and approved before implementation; otherwise keep it local-only.
- Never invent milestone, vaccination or growth guidance; all of it comes from reviewed CMS content or licensed reference data.
- Suppress celebratory content and notifications whenever the outcome is not a birth.
- Additive migrations with tests; OpenAPI kept in sync.
- Run relevant format, lint, static analysis, unit/integration/UI tests and builds before completion.

## Out of scope
- Partner or family sharing.
- Consultation (M21) and nutrition programs (M22).
- Any diagnosis from growth, feeding or mood data.

## Acceptance criteria
- Gate evidence (owner approval and PRD amendment) is linked in the handoff.
- Domain tests: percentile computation matches WHO reference fixtures at boundary values.
- State-machine tests: postpartum is reachable only from a confirmed birth with opt-in, and never after loss or termination.
- Migration tests; sync/export behaviour matches the approved spec.
- `SensitiveAnalyticsGuardTest` covers all new events.
- Goldens (light/dark × 1.0/1.3, RTL) under `<module>/src/test/screenshots/` for the baby home, feeding log, growth chart and milestones.
- All existing checks pass.
