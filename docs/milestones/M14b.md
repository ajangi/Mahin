**Status:** in review (PR #33, gatekeeper round 2)

## Summary

- **63** stroke-based Mahin vector icons (8 families) in `:core:designsystem`, `MahinIcons` / `MahinIcon`, unique fa content descriptions.
- Debug **icon catalogue** activity + composables in `app/src/debug`.
- **`MahinIllustration`** with bundled editorial placeholders and medical gating; test fixtures in unit tests only.
- Roborazzi icon-sheet goldens (families × light/dark + RTL compare light/dark).
