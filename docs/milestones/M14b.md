- **63** original vector icons across **8** families in `:core:designsystem`, typed `MahinIcons` API, fa content descriptions, `MahinIcon` composable (decorative supported).
- Debug **icon catalogue** in `app/src/debug` (not linked from production shell).

- Roborazzi **icon-sheet goldens** (8 families × light/dark + RTL directional sheet) under `android/app/src/test/screenshots/`.
